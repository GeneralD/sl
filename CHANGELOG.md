# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-25

First release. A 3D steam locomotive drives across your desktop, over
your other windows.

- Procedural SceneKit locomotive with animated wheels and drive rods
- Click-through transparent overlay at screen-saver level; smoke
  particles, headlight, ghost steam trail on exit, Dock hop
- Classic flag surface: `-a -c -d -e -F -G -l -w -<n> -v`, plus
  `--all` for every display and `--model` for USDZ/SCN model swaps
- Car/wagon coupling with `-<n>` consist length
- Whistle, chuff, and screams audio (CC0 assets bundled)
- `make install`, man page, GitHub Release tarball, `sl3d` Homebrew
  formula
